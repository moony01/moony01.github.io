# Marketing Brief: Products + Portfolio Main

## Click Hook
Moony01 Studio should give visitors two fast paths: “재밌는 테스트를 바로 해본다” and “이 사람이 실제로 무엇을 만들었는지 확인한다.” The hook is not a generic studio claim. It is a visible split between public products and compact proof of delivery.

Primary hook copy:
> 테스트 제품은 바로 눌러보고, 포트폴리오는 구축 범위와 증거만 빠르게 확인하세요.

## Curiosity Gap
- Products: “내 얼굴상, 두뇌 유형, 정신 연령은 어떻게 나올까?”처럼 즉시 결과를 보고 싶은 curiosity를 만든다.
- Portfolio: “이 프로젝트가 실제로 어떤 범위까지 구현됐고, 어떤 근거로 말할 수 있나?”를 case card 안에서 열어둔다.
- The hub should show enough detail to earn the next click, but not pretend full case pages already exist.

## User Psychology
- Fun-test visitors want low friction, safe links, and recognizable thumbnails.
- Portfolio visitors distrust vague “AI/자동화 구축” claims. They need scope, architecture, evidence, and mask-check status.
- Recruiters or partners scan quickly. They care about role, constraints, visible outputs, and whether sensitive data is handled responsibly.
- Existing blog readers already trust technical writing, so the portfolio link should feel like a natural proof layer beside the blog.

## Distribution Angle
- Blog hero CTA: add `/portfolio/` as a proof-oriented second action.
- Footer Quick Links: add Portfolio so product traffic and article traffic can discover work samples.
- Social share angle: “재밌는 테스트 제품과 실제 구축 사례를 한 페이지에서 보는 개인 스튜디오 포트폴리오.”
- Search angle: “Moony01 Studio 제품”, “AI 테스트”, “멀티 에이전트 운영환경”, “n8n workflow pack”처럼 product and build keywords should coexist.

## Success Criteria
- `/products/`: product clicks increase from the home Products section by giving each product a clearer promise and route.
- `/portfolio/`: visitors understand the four representative cases in under 30 seconds.
- Trust criteria: every portfolio card includes scope, proof/evidence cue, and mask-check state.
- Design criteria: desktop and 390px mobile screenshots pass with no text overflow, no overlap, and >= 16px side padding.
